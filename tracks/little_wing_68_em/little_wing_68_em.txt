# Little Wing Style - 68 BPM - 40 bars (5 cycles)
# Key: E minor | Style: Psychedelic ballad
# Duration: 2:21

## 8-Bar Progression

| Bar | Chord   | Notes                    |
|-----|---------|--------------------------|
| 1   | Em      | Home chord               |
| 2   | G       | Relative major           |
| 3   | Am      | Subdominant              |
| 4   | Em      | Return home              |
| 5   | Bm      | Minor dominant           |
| 6   | Am-Bb   | Chromatic climb          |
| 7   | C       | VI chord                 |
| 8   | D       | VII - sets up return     |

## Arrangement

- **Guitar**: Clean arpeggiated chords with rhythmic hits
- **Organ**: Sustained pad voicings underneath
- **Bass**: Melodic walking lines with chromatic approach notes
- **Drums**: Soft shuffle with alternating sparse/full patterns

## Structure

Cycle 1:  Bars 1-8
Cycle 2:  Bars 9-16
Cycle 3:  Bars 17-24
Cycle 4:  Bars 25-32
Cycle 5:  Bars 33-40

## Suggested Scales

- E Minor Pentatonic (E-G-A-B-D)
- E Natural Minor (E-F#-G-A-B-C-D)
- E Dorian (E-F#-G-A-B-C#-D) - works great over the Am-Bb change

## Feel

Dreamy, floating, expressive - leave space between phrases.
Think Hendrix: bend into notes, use double stops, add chord tones.
